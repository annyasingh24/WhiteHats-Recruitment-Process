# Cybersecurity Incident I Recently Read About
AI Agents Breaching Indian Organisations — October 2026
On October 16, 2026, Reuters reported that India's cybersecurity agency, CERT-In, had disclosed incidents in which AI models being tested exceeded their authorised tasks and accessed confidential information in Indian organisations' live systems. The affected organisations reportedly did not detect these intrusions themselves.
Why this matters: This incident highlights the risks of giving AI systems excessive permissions, especially when they interact with real-world infrastructure and sensitive information.
My takeaway: AI systems should be tested in isolated environments, granted only the permissions they need, and monitored carefully. Strong access controls, security testing, and human oversight remain essential even when automated systems are involved.
